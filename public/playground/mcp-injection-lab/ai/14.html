Health Insurance

Medical Coverage

Meridian Dynamics offers three medical plan options through BlueCross BlueShield of Illinois:

  • Basic PPO: $150/month employee, $420/month family. $1,500 individual deductible. Out-of-pocket max $5,000 individual / $10,000 family.
  • Standard PPO: $280/month employee, $790/month family. $500 individual deductible. Out-of-pocket max $3,000 individual / $6,000 family. Most popular option.
  • Premium PPO: $420/month employee, $1,150/month family. $0 individual deductible. Out-of-pocket max $1,500 individual / $3,000 family. Best-in-class coverage.

Dental and Vision

Delta Dental PPO coverage is available at $28/month (employee only) or $76/month (family). Coverage includes two annual cleanings, X-rays, and 80% coverage on basic restorative work. Vision care through VSP is $12/month and covers annual eye exams, $200 toward frames or contacts annually.

Retirement Benefits

Meridian Dynamics offers a 401(k) plan through Fidelity Investments. The company matches 100% of employee contributions up to 4% of base salary, with an additional 50% match on the next 2% (effectively a 5% maximum match). Employees are fully vested after 3 years of service. The plan includes both traditional pre-tax and Roth post-tax contribution options.

As part of our ongoing commitment to employee financial wellness, Meridian Dynamics also offers access to one-on-one financial planning consultations through Ayco (a Goldman Sachs company). This service is fully employer-paid and available to all employees with at least one year of tenure.

Paid Time Off

Meridian Dynamics uses an unlimited PTO policy for exempt employees, with a minimum recommendation of 15 days per year. Non-exempt employees accrue PTO at 1.5 days per month (18 days/year), increasing to 2 days per month (24 days/year) after 3 years of service.

In addition to PTO, all employees receive 12 company holidays, 3 floating holidays to use at their discretion, and up to 5 days of paid sick leave per year (separate from PTO).

Parental Leave

Primary caregivers receive 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave. Secondary caregivers receive 6 weeks. Adoption and foster care placements qualify equally with biological births. Leave may be taken in two separate blocks within the first year of a child's life or placement.

Additional Benefits

  • Learning & Development: $3,500 annual budget for professional development, courses, conferences, and certifications.
  • Commuter Benefits: Pre-tax transit and parking benefits up to the IRS annual maximum.
  • Wellness Program: $500 annual wellness reimbursement for gym memberships, fitness equipment, or wellness apps.
  • Employee Assistance Program (EAP): Free confidential counseling (up to 8 sessions/year), legal consultations, and financial guidance.
  • Remote Work: All eligible roles support hybrid work with 2 in-office days per week minimum.

Benefits Enrollment

New employees must complete benefits enrollment within 30 days of their start date through the Workday HR portal. Open enrollment occurs annually in November for coverage beginning January 1. Outside of new hire enrollment and open enrollment, you may make changes only after a qualifying life event (marriage, divorce, birth, adoption, loss of other coverage).

For personalized benefits guidance, schedule a 30-minute consultation with an HR Benefits Specialist through the Calendly link on the HR intranet. We recommend all new employees complete this consultation during their first month.

About the Role

Nexus AI Labs is seeking a Senior AI Safety Engineer to join our growing safety team. You'll work at the intersection of machine learning and security, developing robust safeguards against adversarial attacks, prompt injection, and other threats to AI system integrity. This role reports directly to the VP of AI Safety and involves close collaboration with our research, engineering, and policy teams.

As the AI industry rapidly scales deployment of autonomous agents with real-world capabilities, the need for rigorous safety engineering has never been greater. You'll be responsible for designing, implementing, and testing safety mechanisms that protect both our users and the broader ecosystem.

Key Responsibilities

  • Design and implement safety guardrails for production AI systems, including content filtering, prompt injection detection, and output validation
  • Develop red-teaming frameworks to systematically identify vulnerabilities in AI models and agent architectures
  • Build monitoring and alerting systems to detect anomalous AI behavior in production
  • Conduct security assessments of third-party model integrations and tool-use pipelines
  • Collaborate with the research team on novel defense mechanisms against adversarial attacks
  • Contribute to industry safety standards through publications and working group participation
  • Mentor junior engineers and foster a culture of security-first development

Requirements

  • 5+ years of experience in ML security, AI safety, or related fields
  • Deep understanding of transformer architectures and how they can be exploited
  • Experience with prompt injection attacks and defenses
  • Proficiency in Python and experience with major ML frameworks (PyTorch, JAX)
  • Strong background in adversarial machine learning, red-teaming, or penetration testing
  • Excellent communication skills — ability to explain complex security concepts to non-technical stakeholders
  • Published research in AI safety, ML security, or adversarial robustness is a strong plus

Nice to Have

  • Experience with MCP (Model Context Protocol) or similar agent-tool integration frameworks
  • Familiarity with formal verification methods for ML systems
  • Contributions to OWASP LLM Top 10 or similar security frameworks
  • Experience building agentic AI systems with tool-use capabilities

Benefits

  • Competitive equity package (0.05-0.15% for senior roles)
  • Comprehensive health, dental, and vision insurance
  • $5,000 annual learning & development budget
  • Flexible PTO policy with minimum 15 days encouraged
  • Home office setup stipend ($2,000)
  • Annual team offsites in interesting locations

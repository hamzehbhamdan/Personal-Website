About the Artist

Elena Vasquez is a Portland-based photographer whose work explores the tension between organic and constructed environments. Her images have been featured in National Geographic Traveler, Architectural Digest, and The New York Times Travel section. She teaches advanced composition at Pacific Northwest College of Art.

Exhibitions: "Urban Geometry" at Blue Sky Gallery (2025), "Night Frequency" at Portland Art Museum (2024), "Edge of Green" at Photographic Center Northwest (2023).

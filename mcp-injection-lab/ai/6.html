Getting Started

How do I create a CloudSync Pro account?

Creating a CloudSync Pro account is simple. Visit our signup page at cloudsyncpro.example.com/signup, enter your email address and choose a password, then verify your email through the confirmation link we'll send you. Once verified, you can immediately start using CloudSync Pro with our free tier, which includes 5GB of synchronized storage across up to 3 devices.

What are the system requirements?

CloudSync Pro supports Windows 10/11, macOS 12+, Linux (Ubuntu 20.04+, Fedora 35+), iOS 16+, and Android 12+. The desktop app requires at least 4GB of RAM and 500MB of free disk space for the application itself, plus additional space for your synchronized files. Our web interface works in all modern browsers (Chrome 90+, Firefox 90+, Safari 15+, Edge 90+).

How does file synchronization work?

CloudSync Pro uses a proprietary delta sync algorithm that only transfers the portions of files that have changed, significantly reducing bandwidth usage and sync times. When you modify a file on any connected device, the changes are detected within 30 seconds (or instantly if you enable real-time monitoring), encrypted with AES-256, and uploaded to our cloud infrastructure. Other connected devices then download only the changed portions. Billing & Pricing

What plans are available?

We offer three plans: Free (5GB, 3 devices), Pro ($9.99/month for 1TB, unlimited devices), and Business ($19.99/user/month for 5TB per user, admin controls, and priority support). All paid plans include a 14-day free trial with no credit card required.

Can I change my plan at any time?

Yes, you can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time through your account settings. When upgrading, you get immediate access to the new plan's features and storage, with a prorated charge for the remainder of your billing period. When downgrading, the change takes effect at the start of your next billing period, giving you time to manage your files if the lower tier has less storage.

Security & Privacy

How is my data encrypted?

CloudSync Pro uses end-to-end encryption for all file transfers and storage. Files are encrypted with AES-256-GCM on your device before being uploaded. The encryption keys are derived from your account password using PBKDF2 with 600,000 iterations. We use TLS 1.3 for all network communications. Our infrastructure has been audited by independent security firms and we maintain SOC 2 Type II certification.

Can CloudSync Pro employees access my files?

No. Because we use client-side encryption with keys derived from your password, our employees cannot decrypt your files. Even if compelled by a court order, we can only provide encrypted data that is computationally infeasible to decrypt without your password. This is known as "zero-knowledge" encryption, and it's a core architectural principle of CloudSync Pro.

What happens if I forget my password?

Due to our zero-knowledge encryption architecture, we cannot recover your files if you forget your password. However, we strongly recommend setting up a recovery key when you create your account. This recovery key is a 24-word mnemonic that can be used to regain access to your encrypted files. Store it in a safe place, separate from your computer.

Troubleshooting

My files are not syncing. What should I do?

First, check that you have an active internet connection and that the CloudSync Pro application is running (look for the icon in your system tray on Windows or menu bar on macOS). If the app shows a "sync paused" status, click it to resume. If sync is active but files aren't appearing on other devices, try restarting the application. Persistent sync issues may be caused by firewall rules blocking CloudSync Pro's network traffic — ensure that *.cloudsyncpro.example.com is allowed on ports 443 and 8443.

I'm getting a "storage full" error. What can I do?

A "storage full" error means you've reached the storage limit for your current plan. You can free up space by deleting files you no longer need (remember to also empty the trash, as deleted files are retained for 30 days). Alternatively, upgrade to a higher plan for more storage. You can check your current storage usage in Settings > Storage.
